# 01 — Product Vision

## Vision
Build an open, evidence-driven intelligence layer that converts scattered citizen needs into ranked infrastructure priorities that policymakers can understand and act upon.

## Product Promise
**Listen to communities. Correlate evidence. Understand infrastructure gaps. Prioritize transparently.**

## Current-to-New Transformation

### Old Nivaran
Citizen complaint
→ evidence validation
→ clustering
→ impact
→ government complaint/escalation

### New Nivaran
Citizen voice/text/photo
→ demand understanding
→ evidence trust
→ demand clustering
→ contextual data fusion
→ priority scoring
→ policy reasoning
→ human decision

## Core Value
Nivaran does not merely tell government what citizens reported.
It helps answer:
**“Given what communities are asking for and what the region already has or funds, what should policymakers examine first?”**

## Product Principles
1. Evidence before narrative.
2. Decision support before dashboard decoration.
3. Deterministic metrics before LLM arithmetic.
4. Human authority remains human.
5. Country-neutral core, country-specific adapters.
6. Honest uncertainty.
7. Reuse proven Nivaran capabilities.
8. Ship one complete loop before adding breadth.

## Non-Goals for MVP
- fully integrated government procurement
- automatic budget approval
- automatic government submission
- full national digital identity integration
- all BRICS languages
- real-time data from every government system
- predictive national forecasting
- native mobile application
- large-scale production infrastructure beyond prototype needs
