## Category Engineering Interpretations

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Official requirements strictly dictate scoring criteria. Engineering strategies describe how the project maximizes score under each official criterion.

### 1. Problem-Solution Fit (20%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Clear alignment between consolidated citizen demand signals and national infrastructure planning gaps.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Shift hero view from individual complaint tickets to **Demand Hotspots / Demand Clusters** paired directly with national demographic/infrastructure data for planner decision support.

### 2. AI/Technical Execution (25%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Meaningful, non-decorative Google AI integration performing complex multimodal structuring, translation, reasoning, and policy recommendation generation.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Use Gemini 2.5 for structured JSON multimodal demand classification, cross-border voice/text translation, and evidence-grounded Policy Action Brief synthesis. Keep math, spatial clustering, and priority scoring strictly deterministic.

### 3. Cross-Border Applicability (20%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Multilingual capability and multi-country scalability across BRICS nations.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Provide turnkey country adapters for **India, Brazil, and South Africa**, demonstrating dynamic schema translation, currency/census formatting, and regional language support (Hindi, Portuguese, Zulu/English).

### 4. Deployability & Scalability (20%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Production-ready code, live deployed application link, containerized architecture, and robust evidence validation.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Zero-downtime multi-stage Docker setup, clean separation of API layers, verified fast response latency, and complete local/cloud deployment parity.

### 5. Impact Potential (10%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Measurable improvement in public spending alignment and vulnerable population coverage.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Embed deterministic priority scoring incorporating vulnerable population density indices, verified waiting days, and active signal density.

### 6. Presentation & Clarity (5%)
- **OFFICIAL REQUIREMENT**: Concise, compelling 3–5 minute demo video, 10–12 slide pitch deck, and clear value proposition.
- **OUR STRATEGY FOR MAXIMIZING SCORE**: Provide step-by-step interactive policymaker intelligence workflow with clear visual demand maps, evidence ledgers, and explainable AI policy recommendations.

---

## Non-Negotiable Scoring Requirements
1. **Google AI Integration**: Gemini must do heavy lifting (multimodal understanding, cross-border translation, policy synthesis); simple wrapper calls will forfeit points.
2. **End-to-End Prototype**: Every button, view, and workflow shown in the demo must be backed by real API responses and deterministic computations.
3. **Cross-Border Live Proof**: Multi-country switching must change data schemas, languages, and census overlays dynamically on screen.
4. **No Fabricated Facts**: Demo data must be visibly tagged as demo fixtures (`is_demo: True`); priority scores and aggregate metrics must be calculated deterministically.
5. **Live Deployment**: The app must be accessible via public HTTPS URL during evaluation.
