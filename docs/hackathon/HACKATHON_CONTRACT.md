# HACKATHON CONTRACT — Immutable Source of Truth

## A. Hackathon Name
**Build with AI: Code for Communities — Second Edition**

## B. Track
**Track 1 — AI for Digital Public Infrastructure & Governance**

## C. BRICS Pillar / Theme
**BRICS Theme: Innovation**

## D. Official Problem Statement
> “Governments across BRICS nations struggle to consolidate citizen feedback and align it with national infrastructure priorities. Development requests live in fragmented systems, leading to misaligned public spending, unaddressed infrastructure gaps, and no way to measure the impact of large-scale digital public infrastructure initiatives.”

## E. Official Challenge Statement
> “Build a scalable, multilingual AI platform — designed as a Digital Public Good — that aggregates citizen development requests via voice, text, and messaging apps across diverse linguistic regions. The system should analyse large datasets combining citizen feedback with national demographic data, infrastructure indices, and public investment plans, surfacing demand hotspots and recommending high-priority development projects to national policymakers across BRICS nations.”

## F. Build Requirements
- **Core End-to-End Flow**: Functioning, non-stubbed execution flow from intake to policy recommendation.
- **Mandatory AI Integration**: Google AI (Gemini) must perform meaningful analysis, structuring, multimodal understanding, and reasoning.
- **Data Foundation**: Real or realistic datasets combining citizen signals with national demographics and infrastructure indices.
- **Cross-Border Applicability**: Architecture must support multi-country/multilingual adaptation without hardcoded India-only assumptions.
- **Multilingual / Voice Support**: Intake and policy outputs must support voice and diverse regional languages.

## G. Submission Requirements
- Source Code Repository
- 3–5 Minute Demo Video
- 10–12 Slide Pitch Deck
- Short Product Description & Value Proposition
- Deployed Live Application URL

## H. Published Hackathon Rules
1. **Google AI Mandatory**: Google AI tools (Gemini 2.5, Google AI SDK) are mandatory for core AI operations.
2. **Substantial Extension**: Pre-existing projects must be substantially transformed and extended for the challenge.
3. **Intellectual Property**: All submitted code must be original or open-source licensed under compatible licenses.
4. **Cross-Border Scalability**: Solutions must be designed for multi-region or cross-border applicability across BRICS nations.
5. **Finality**: Judges' decisions are final.
